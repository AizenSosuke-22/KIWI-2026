# Positioning Statement — Kivi

Kivi is currently viable only on laptops and desktops. Within that constraint, the initial market is the 14 to 50 cohort with laptop access: high school and college students, and corporate professionals looking to boost productivity. The long-term market is rural India, where fluent speech far outpaces comfortable writing, a gap Kivi can uniquely bridge. Against established products like Wispr Flow, Kivi cannot yet compete on feature count, but leads clearly on Indian dialect and pronunciation accuracy, and on regional language coverage. Judiciary, government dictation work, hospitals, and the education sector are the highest-potential adjacent markets.
